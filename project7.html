Architecture REST API + React SPA (Client-Server Architecture)

Problem

Many small shops track stock manually which leads to inaccurate inventory, lost sales data and poor supplier management.

Solution

A lightweight SaaS system allowing shop owners to manage inventory, record sales, track supplier purchases and manage customer credit from a single dashboard.

Key Features

  • Multi-branch stock tracking
  • POS sales system
  • Supplier purchase management
  • Customer credit tracking
  • Stock history & analytics
