Hospital Management System

A full-stack healthcare dashboard designed to manage patients, appointments, billing and hospital inventory operations through a centralized digital system.

Problem

Many small and mid-size healthcare facilities rely on fragmented systems or manual paperwork to track patient records, appointments and billing, which leads to inefficiencies and data management challenges.

Solution

A centralized hospital management platform that digitizes patient records, appointment scheduling, billing operations and inventory tracking within a secure role-based system.

Key Features

  • Patient record management
  • Appointment scheduling system
  • Secure role-based authentication
  • Billing and revenue analytics dashboard
  • Medical inventory management
