What I build, and what I build it with

Backend and full-stack engineering for systems that carry real business logic: multi-tenant SaaS, payment and notification infrastructure, asynchronous processing, and the containerised deployments that keep them running.

Capabilities

How I work

  • Multi-tenant SaaS platforms

    Organisation-scoped data isolation, role-based access across backend and frontend, subscription billing, credit systems and reporting that holds up as tenants grow.

  • Backend APIs & system architecture

    Modular REST APIs with authentication, relational data modelling, versioned migrations and clean separation between API requests, workers and persistence.

  • Payments & financial workflows

    M-Pesa and Stripe integrations, payroll statutory deductions, salary advances, salary disbursement, customer credit tracking and reconciliation.

  • Event-driven & asynchronous processing

    Redis and Celery workers handling notifications, invoice transmission and heavy reporting outside the request path, with retries and delivery tracking.

  • Production infrastructure & support

    Docker Compose deployments on Ubuntu with Nginx or Caddy, HTTPS, persistent storage and backups — plus monitoring, health checks, structured logging and production debugging.

  • Enterprise systems & collaboration

    ERPNext and Frappe configuration, business process reengineering and approval workflows — delivered in Agile teams with code reviews and stakeholder collaboration.
